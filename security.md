## Signed announcements

When an announcement is accompanied by a PGP signature, verify that signature with the public key above. Do not treat an unsigned announcement as cryptographically verified.

## Responsible disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability affecting BitDevs Maputo or its services, please report it privately. Encrypt your report with the public key above and email it to [bitdevsmaputo@gmail.com](mailto:bitdevsmaputo@gmail.com). Include enough detail to reproduce the issue, but avoid accessing, changing, or sharing other people's data.
