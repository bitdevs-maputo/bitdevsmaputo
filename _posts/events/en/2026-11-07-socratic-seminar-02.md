## About the event

At the second BitDevs Maputo Socratic Seminar, we explored **"Don't Trust, Verify"**: instead of trusting blindly, we seek to understand, question, and verify.

How are private keys generated? How can a wallet derive multiple addresses from a recovery phrase? The event welcomed students, developers, Bitcoin enthusiasts, and anyone curious about how wallets work.

## Topics

- [BIP-32](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0032.mediawiki): hierarchical derivation of keys and addresses.
- [BIP-39](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0039.mediawiki): mnemonic phrases and wallet recovery.
- [BIP-44](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0044.mediawiki): account organization and derivation paths.
- Security and self-custody: control of funds and the risks of trusting a wallet without verification.
