## Announcements

We are pleased to invite you to **Back to 2008 - The Beginning of the Revolution**!

Before there was a price, there was an idea.

In 2008, amid one of the greatest financial crises in history, a person or group under the pseudonym **Satoshi Nakamoto** published a nine-page document titled **Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System**.

That document introduced a proposal that would transform the way we think about money, trust, and decentralized systems.

In this meetup, we will go back to 2008 and explore the **Bitcoin Whitepaper** from the beginning, seeking to understand the problems Bitcoin aimed to solve and the mechanisms used to build a peer-to-peer electronic cash system.

No technical background is required. The goal is to create a space to learn, question, and understand the technology behind Bitcoin.
