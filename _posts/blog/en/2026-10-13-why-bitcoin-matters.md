## Why Bitcoin matters

Bitcoin matters because it gives people a way to move value without asking for permission from a central authority.

### Practical reasons

- It is open and transparent.
- It is borderless and censorship resistant.
- It can be verified by anyone running a node.

### The real question

The important discussion is not whether Bitcoin is perfect, but whether it gives people more optionality, autonomy and economic freedom than the systems we currently rely on.
