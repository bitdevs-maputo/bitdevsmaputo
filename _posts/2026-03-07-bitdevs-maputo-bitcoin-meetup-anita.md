# Announcements

Join us for the inaugural BitDevs Meetup in Mozambique, held in Maputo as part of Bitcoin Week in Mozambique 2026.

On March 7, 2026, Bitcoin for Fairness hosted the BitDevs Maputo Kick-off Workshop, bringing together 70 participants to introduce the BitDevs format and create a foundation for a regular Bitcoin technical community in Mozambique.

A special thank you to Bitcoin for Fairness and Bitcoin Famba for supporting the development of the Bitcoin community in Mozambique.
